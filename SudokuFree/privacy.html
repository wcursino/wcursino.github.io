Sudoku Free – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Sudoku Free is free to play and supported by advertising. This page explains what data is involved.

Data the app itself keeps

Game progress, statistics and settings are stored only on your device. The developer does not operate any server and does not receive this data.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your solve times and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center service so they can appear on leaderboards under your Game Center nickname. This is handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. You can play without signing in; rankings and achievements are then unavailable.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows a banner advertisement provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google's SDK may collect device information such as an advertising identifier, IP address and approximate location, device model and OS version, and information about your interaction with ads. This data is processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

On first use the app asks for permission to track via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised using your advertising identifier. You can change this any time in iOS Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Tracking, and reset your advertising identifier in Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Apple Advertising. You can also opt out of personalised ads from Google at adssettings.google.com.

Children

The app is rated 4+ but is not directed at children. If you believe a child has provided data through the app, contact the address below.
