How can we help?

Questions, feedback, or a problem with the app? Contact the developer directly.

Email support

wilson.cursino@gmail.com

Report a study-content issue

Include the exact question, category or module, answer shown, and the correction you suggest. Where possible, identify the aircraft variant and the relevant manual section and revision. Please do not send restricted manuals or sensitive personal information.

Report a technical problem

Include your app version, device model, operating-system version, and the steps that led to the issue. A screenshot with personal information removed can help. In-app feedback includes app and device details so we can troubleshoot.

Does the app work offline?

Core quizzes, flashcards, study guides, and local progress work offline. Checking for updates, opening external links, and sending email require an internet connection.

How do I update?

Open the app's Settings and choose Check for Updates, or visit its App Store page. Updates become available after Apple releases them and may depend on your operating-system version and storefront.

Where is my progress stored?

Study progress and preferences are stored locally on your device. There is no developer-operated account or study-history sync service. Deleting the app can remove local progress; device backups and restoration are handled by Apple.
