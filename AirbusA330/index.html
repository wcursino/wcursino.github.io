Airbus A330 Systems

Prepare for your A330 type rating with system quizzes, oral prep, simulator guides, and hands-free audio study—all in one app.

View on the App Store

Study your way

Build your knowledge for initial, recurrent, or return-to-line training with A330 Prep. Explore more than 1,000 multiple-choice and oral questions across aircraft systems, with explanations to help you understand each answer.

  • Systems and oral prep: quizzes, flashcards, memory items, limitations, and checkride practice.
  • Smart Review: spaced repetition to revisit material that needs more practice.
  • Simulator preparation: maneuver guides, cockpit familiarization, flows, and task sharing.
  • Hands-free study: Podcast Mode reads study material aloud and supports lock-screen playback controls.
  • Track your progress: review your accuracy by system, earn XP, and build a study streak.

Ready when you are

Core study features work offline, with no account, advertising, or subscriptions. Update checks, App Store links, and sending support email require an internet connection.

Help improve the study material

Found a question that needs a correction? Send the exact question and its category through A330 support. Pilot feedback helps improve the accuracy and consistency of the content.

Independent study companion

A330 Prep is not affiliated with or endorsed by Airbus or any airline. Always defer to current approved FCOM, FCTM, and company procedures for your aircraft.
