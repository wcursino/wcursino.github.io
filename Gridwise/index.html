Gridwise: Learn Sudoku

Coming soon to the App Store

The Sudoku that teaches you how to solve it. iPhone and iPad. Free. Plays offline.

Most Sudoku apps help by filling in a number. Gridwise shows you why. Tap Coach and it finds the next logical step on your board, highlights the cells involved, explains the reasoning in plain words, and lets you apply it or try it yourself.

A coach, not just hints

  • A nudge first ("look at where 7 can go in box 5"), then the full explanation if you want it
  • The pattern drawn on your board: the cells involved, the digit to place, the candidates to remove
  • Twelve techniques: Full House, Naked and Hidden Singles, Pointing, Claiming, Pairs, Triples, X-Wing, XY-Wing and Swordfish
  • Made a mistake? The coach spots it first, so you never learn from a wrong board
  • A Technique Guide with a lesson for each technique

Difficulty that means something

  • Every puzzle is graded by the hardest technique it needs, not by how many numbers are given
  • Each level adds new techniques, from Easy (singles) to Master (XY-Wing and Swordfish)
  • Win to unlock the next level. The win card shows which techniques you just used

Three ways to play, plus a daily puzzle

  • Classic, Diagonal (both long diagonals hold 1 to 9) and Jigsaw (a new region shape every game)
  • A Daily Puzzle, the same for everyone, with streaks and a result you can share
  • Every puzzle is generated on your device and has exactly one solution, so you never run out

Made for comfort

  • Pencil notes (the coach can fill them in), undo, highlighting and a long-press radial number picker
  • Light, dark or automatic theme, sound effects and haptics
  • iPad layout with the board and keypad side by side in landscape, plus hardware keyboard shortcuts
  • Optional Game Center rankings and achievements

Free, supported by ads

Gridwise has no subscriptions, no in-app purchases and no account. It is supported by a single Google AdMob banner below the board. You can also watch an optional video when you want the coach to place a digit for you. The coach's explanations are always free. Puzzles work fully offline; ads and rankings need a connection.
