How can we help?

Questions, feedback or a problem with the app? Contact the developer directly.

Email support

wilson.cursino@gmail.com

Report a problem

Please include your app version, device model, iOS version, what you expected and what happened. If useful, attach a screenshot with personal information removed.

Report a card that needs correcting

Include the aircraft, the system, the question and the part you believe needs correction. Each card shows its source reference (for example "FlightSafety AG-1" or "Foltz p.37"); please include it. Always use current approved operational documentation.

Which aircraft does it cover?

The Gulfstream G450 and G550. Choose your aircraft with the Deck card on the Study screen; each deck holds only the cards that apply to that type. Facts common to both types appear in both decks and share your progress.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Every deck, quiz mode and your progress work offline. Advertising requires a connection.

Where is my progress?

XP, ranks, streaks, achievements and card statistics are stored on this device. The app does not provide account-based sync. Deleting the app removes its local data, subject to device backups and Apple's restore behavior. Offloading the app may retain its data. Reset All Progress on the Profile screen clears it.

Advertising

A Google AdMob banner appears at the bottom of active quiz, flashcard and Checkride screens, including Checkride results. It sits in its own space below the questions, answers and cards, so it never covers study content. The Study, Browse and Profile screens and other quiz results have no banner. Study continues normally if an ad cannot load.

Privacy choices

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and other regions where it is required, the app asks for your advertising choices before showing ads. To review or change them later, tap Privacy Choices at the top of the Study screen. The button appears only where the consent service requires it. Study is available whatever you choose. See the Privacy Policy for how advertising uses information.
