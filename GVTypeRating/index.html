G-V Type Rating

Study for your Gulfstream G450 and G550 type rating with flashcards, quizzes and spaced repetition that brings back what you're about to forget.

Coming soon to the App Store

One deck for your aircraft.

Pick Gulfstream G450 or Gulfstream G550 and study everything that applies to that type, organized across 28 systems from Electrical and Powerplant to Autoflight, Avionics and SOP.

  • Gulfstream G550 (729 cards): G550 limitations (speeds, weights, altitudes, engine, fuel, icing and autopilot), the FlightSafety G550 flashcard set and the G550 systems notes.
  • Gulfstream G450 (619 cards): the FlightSafety G450 flashcard set and the G450 systems notes.
  • Sources: FlightSafety flashcards, with the original diagrams where they matter, and nearly 600 questions from Steven Foltz's Expanded Notes, both used with permission.

Five ways to study.

  • Flashcards: flip, then rate yourself Again, Hard, Good or Easy.
  • Multiple choice: pick the right answer from options drawn from the same system.
  • Type answer: recall values from memory.
  • Checkride: a timed exam of 10 to 100 questions with an 80% pass mark. No feedback until you submit, then review every miss.
  • Limitations & Memory Items: drill only the must-knows, AFM limits, other published limits and immediate-action memory items, in any of the formats above.

Swipe left for the next question and right to go back.

Built to stick.

Spaced repetition schedules every card for review just before you forget it, and weak cards come back more often. Earn XP, climb from Student Pilot to Chief Pilot, keep a daily streak and unlock achievements. Track mastery for each deck and system.

Private and offline.

No account is required. Study content and progress are stored on your device, and every deck works offline. The app shows a Google AdMob banner at the bottom of active quiz and flashcard screens, in its own space below the questions and cards so it never covers study content. Where required, including in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your advertising choices first, and Privacy Choices on the Study screen lets you change them. Study continues normally if an ad cannot load.
