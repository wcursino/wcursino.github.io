Airbus A320 Systems

Your A320 Checkride Prep companion. Build confidence with focused quizzes, flashcards and a study routine that fits your day.

View on the App Store

Make your next study session count.

  • Quick quizzes: practice across topics or focus on a specific category.
  • Flashcards: review A320/A321 limitations and memory items.
  • Lights & Switches: connect cockpit images with knowledge questions.
  • Smart Review: revisit material through spaced repetition.
  • Checkride Mode: practice with 50 questions, a 60-minute timer and an 80% passing score, then review missed answers.
  • Progress: track accuracy, earn XP and build study streaks.

Study at your own pace.

No account is required. Study content and progress are stored on your device, and core study works offline. In ad-supported versions, banners appear on flashcards, quizzes and completed-session screens; active quizzes have a bottom banner below the scrollable questions and answers. Flashcards have a separate compact bottom banner below the study controls. Smart Review questions and timed exams have no ad placements.

Support development. Study without ads.

Learn, Stats and Settings have no ads. In versions offering in-app purchases, Settings offers weekly and monthly auto-renewable subscriptions to remove all ads while active, or lifetime ad removal with a one-time purchase. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Settings. Buying lifetime access does not cancel an existing subscription. Prices are shown in your local currency by the App Store. Restore Purchases recovers eligible purchases using the same Apple Account.
