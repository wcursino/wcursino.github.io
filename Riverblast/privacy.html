Riverblast – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

In short: Riverblast does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data the app keeps on your device

The game saves your high score and whether sound is switched on. This is stored only on your device using the standard iOS settings storage. The developer does not operate any server and never receives this data. It is deleted when you delete the app.

Network access

Riverblast plays entirely offline and does not connect to the internet.

Game controllers and keyboards

If you connect a game controller or keyboard, its input is read by iOS on your device only to control the game. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Advertising and tracking

This version of Riverblast contains no advertising and no third-party code that collects data, and it does not track you across apps or websites. If a future version adds advertising, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app’s App Store privacy details will be updated to match.

Children

Riverblast is a general-audience game and is not directed at children. Because the app collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

Any changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
