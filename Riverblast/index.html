Riverblast

FLY LOW. BLOW BRIDGES. REFUEL.

Take the controls of a fighter jet racing up an endless river. Blast tankers, helicopters and enemy jets, destroy bridges to reach new checkpoints, and keep an eye on that fuel gauge. Fly over fuel depots to refill, or shoot them for points if you’re feeling brave.

Download on the App Store

Features

  • Chunky early-80s console look: blocky riverbanks, pixel sprites and a classic gauge-style HUD
  • Real-time chiptune sound: engine roar, missile zaps, explosions and a low-fuel alarm
  • Simple, precise controls: virtual joystick to steer and change speed, hold fire to shoot
  • Bridges are checkpoints, and you earn an extra jet every 10,000 points
  • Game controller and keyboard support
  • iPhone and iPad, landscape
  • No account, no tracking, no data collected
