Common questions

How do I fly?
Drag anywhere on the left half of the screen to steer left and right. Push up to fly faster and pull down to slow down. Touch and hold anywhere on the right half to fire. Small movements give fine control, and a bigger push turns faster.

Why did I crash with fuel left?
Touching a riverbank, an island, a bridge or an enemy destroys your jet. Bridges must be shot before you reach them.

How do I refuel?
Fly over a FUEL depot and stay on it. Flying slower over a depot fills more of your tank. Shooting a depot gives 80 points but destroys the fuel. When the gauge drops below a quarter, an alarm sounds.

Where do I restart after losing a jet?
At the last bridge you destroyed. Each bridge you blow up becomes your new checkpoint, with a full tank.

How do I get extra jets?
You earn one every 10,000 points, up to 9 in reserve.

Can I use a game controller or keyboard?
Yes. Controllers: left stick or d-pad to fly, A, B or the right trigger to fire, Menu to pause. Keyboards: arrow keys or WASD to fly, Space to fire, P or Esc to pause.

How do I turn the sound off?
Tap the pause button (top left) and choose Sound: On/Off. Game sound plays even when your iPhone is set to silent, so use this switch or the volume buttons.

How do I reset my high score?
The high score is stored only on your device. Deleting and reinstalling the app resets it.

Does it work on iPad?
Yes, in landscape, on iPad and iPhone.
