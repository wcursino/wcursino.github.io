Phenom 300 Checkride

Prepare for your Phenom 300 checkride with system-by-system quizzes, instant feedback and progress tracking.

View on the App Store

Make your next study session count.

  • 162 questions, 22 systems: quiz all categories at once or focus on one system, from Electrical and Powerplant to AFCS, CAS Messages and Limitations.
  • Instant feedback: tap an answer to see right or wrong immediately, then move on automatically.
  • Flashcards: review questions and answers without scoring.
  • Weak Areas: questions you miss come back more often, and Weak Areas mode focuses on them.
  • Progress: earn XP with speed bonuses, climb six ranks from Student Pilot to Check Pilot, build daily streaks and unlock 17 achievements.
  • Stats: track overall accuracy and mastery for each category.

Study at your own pace.

No account is required. Study content and progress are stored on your device, and quizzes and flashcards work offline. The app shows a Google AdMob banner at the bottom of active quiz and flashcard screens, in its own space below the questions and cards so it never covers study content. Study continues normally if an ad cannot load.
