How can we help?

Questions, feedback or a problem with the app? Contact the developer directly.

Email support

wilson.cursino@gmail.com

Report a problem

Please include your app version, device model, iOS version, what you expected and what happened. If useful, attach a screenshot with personal information removed.

Report a study-content issue

Include the category, the question and the part you believe needs correction. Describe the applicable source and revision without sending restricted manuals or personal information. Always use current approved operational documentation.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Quizzes, flashcards, Weak Areas and local progress work offline. Advertising and the App Store link require a connection.

Where is my progress?

XP, ranks, streaks, achievements and question statistics are stored on this device. The app does not provide account-based sync. Deleting the app removes its local data, subject to device backups and Apple's restore behavior. Offloading the app may retain its data.

Advertising

A Google AdMob banner appears at the bottom of active quiz and flashcard screens. It sits in its own space below the questions, answers and cards, so it never covers study content. Home, Stats and Results have no banner. Study continues normally if an ad cannot load.

Privacy choices

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and other regions where it is required, the app asks for your advertising choices before showing ads. To review or change them later, tap Privacy Choices at the top of the Home screen. The button appears only where the consent service requires it. Study is available whatever you choose. See the Privacy Policy for how advertising uses information.
