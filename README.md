# Wilson Cursino app website

The root is the app directory. Each app has its own folder: SudokuFree, DivIncome, Riverblast, AirbusA320, AirbusA330, Phenom300, GVTypeRating.

GitHub Pages deploys from main, repository root. Keep app-ads.txt at the root. Root privacy.html and support.html remain Sudoku Free pages for existing App Store links. Their folder copies are under SudokuFree/. Keep both copies in sync when editing policy or support content.

App Store links were verified against Apple’s developer lookup (artist 920514282). Sudoku Free was not returned in the US listing; its existing site remains available, without the old placeholder download link.

Sudoku Free is awaiting App Store review (confirmed by the developer). Keep its Coming soon label until published, then add the verified listing URL. Other icons use Apple’s artwork URLs; Sudoku uses its project icon.

Phenom 300 Checkride uses /Phenom300/ (marketing), /Phenom300/support.html and /Phenom300/privacy.html for its App Store URLs. The older wcursino/phenomcheckride-privacy page is superseded by /Phenom300/privacy.html.

G-V Type Rating (G450/G550) uses /GVTypeRating/ (marketing), /GVTypeRating/support.html and /GVTypeRating/privacy.html for its App Store URLs. Its icon is assets/icons/gv-type-rating.png. Keep its Coming soon label until published, then add the verified listing URL.
